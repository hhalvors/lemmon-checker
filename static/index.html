Symbolic Logic Tools

Companion tools for How Logic Works. Check a natural-deduction proof, evaluate a sentence in a finite structure, or build a truth table.

Proofs

Proof checker Type a proof and have every line checked against the rules. Check a photo of a proof Photograph a proof you wrote by hand. You confirm the reading before it is checked. Blank template A printable sheet to write proofs on. PDF.

Sentences and models

Truth tables Every valuation of a propositional sentence, and its value under each. DNF converter Rewrite a propositional sentence in disjunctive normal form. Model checker Build a finite structure and evaluate a closed sentence in it. Graphical model builder Draw a structure and read off what holds in it.
